A module prints a `fold` over the targets before the origin as a scan (COMPILE.MODULE.SCAN). The test of such a fold compares a value of the target with a key of the origin by `<` or `<=`. A run evaluates that value once and each step at most once at each record, not at each origin. Thus the angles of the radial module cost O(n log n), not O(n²). The values and the errors do not change.
