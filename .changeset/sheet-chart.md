The sheet app draws a chart of the columns that you choose. A column of numbers gives a line across the rows or across a column of numbers. A column of vectors gives a path with one scale on the two axes. A click on a point selects its row. The chart is a part of the document, so undo, the share link and the JSON file keep it. The loan, the rabbits and foxes, and the thrown ball open with a chart.
