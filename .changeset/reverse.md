The axis `reverse(axis)` has the targets of an axis in the reverse order, from the last to the first (AXIS.REVERSE). The inner axis is a base axis: not `where`, `reverse` or `table`. A fold over it starts at the last target, for example to pack a row from its last child. The builder has the option `reverse: true` of each axis. Tier 2 and a standalone module loop from the end, and the reference interpreter and the random programs of the test kit have the axis too.
