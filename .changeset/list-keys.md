A list chain has `keys()` and `errors()` (spec BUILD.LIST.KEYS). `keys()` gives the keys of the items with a value, in the order of the axis, and `errors()` gives the error of each item that failed. The IR had both list ops, and the builder now offers them. The Lab reads them too.
